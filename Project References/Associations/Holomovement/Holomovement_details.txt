holomovement.net is the central hub for The Holomovement, a global social and cultural movement inspired by the physics and philosophy of David Bohm. It is not a traditional organization but rather a "living ecosystem" designed to catalyze a shift in human consciousness toward interconnectedness, coherence, and collaborative action for the good of all life.

Core Philosophy: The Bohmian Connection
The movement takes its name from David Bohm's concept of the holomovement—a term describing the "undivided wholeness" of the universe. Bohm proposed that beneath the fragmented surface of reality lies an implicate order, a deeper, dynamic field where everything is interconnected. The Holomovement applies this physical theory to human culture, suggesting that when individuals recognize this underlying wholeness, cooperation and purposeful action become natural

Key Pillars and Initiatives
The ecosystem is built around several distinct but interconnected programs:

1. The Wave Gatherings The movement organizes high-coherence global events called "The Wave." These gatherings bring together artists, scientists, Indigenous leaders, and innovators who are drawn by a shared commitment to act from unity rather than separation.

Past & Future Events: Sedona (2023), Ibiza (2024), Asheville (2025), and Lisbon (2026)

Purpose: To cultivate deep connection, spark unexpected collaborations, and strengthen a global network dedicated to planetary thriving

2. The Engine for Good This is a regenerative funding cycle described as a "living ecosystem for change."

Mechanism: Members contribute small "sharing gifts" to a pool.
Holons: Groups of 3+ people (called "Holons") collaborate on inspired-action projects.
Microgrants: These projects receive funding from the pool. As they demonstrate results, the cycle strengthens, allowing resources to flow back to the field

Impact: To date, they report distributing over $572K in grants to grassroots initiatives, particularly through partnerships with Purpose Earth

Why It Matters
The movement argues that humanity is at a turning point where systems driven by extraction and competition are failing. They believe the solution lies in a "field of coherence" where individual purpose converges to serve the "good of the whole." Their goal is to help humanity "remember its wholeness" and translate shared values into meaningful, tangible impact
